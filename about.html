About Fuelerz

A Goa-born studio with restless energy.

Fuelerz was created for people who do not want their business to look like everybody else’s. It is a design and digital studio built around websites, identities, graphics, print, campaigns and the small details that make customers take a brand seriously.

The name is a reminder: good design is fuel. It gives an idea movement, confidence and a visible shape.

Founder

Sergio Caetano Fernandes.

Born and raised in Goa, Sergio Caetano Fernandes is the young founder behind Fuelerz. He started the studio from a late-night idea that felt too sharp to ignore: every business has a story, but not every business knows how to make that story visible.

For Sergio, Fuelerz is not only about websites or logos. It is about turning imagination into something tangible — a brand people can recognise, a website people can use, a design people can remember, and a digital presence that gives a business more confidence.

The studio works best with clients who bring vision, trust and ambition. Fuelerz brings the design, structure and execution to help that vision take form.
